We are excited to welcome Hassan to our group this January as a Ph.D. student.  
He completed his Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics in 2018 at the University of Isfahan.

Hassan’s research interests include computer vision , optimization, and probabilistic graphical models. He has also collaborated on text mining projects.

His recent work focuses on developing digital twins and autonomous navigation systems for robots operating in construction environments.
