We are delighted to share that Pavithra has been elected as the Vice Chair of [ACM SIGBED](https://sigbed.org/)
for the term July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2027.

ACM SIGBED is a leading international organization that fosters collaboration and innovation in the field of embedded and cyber-physical systems.
