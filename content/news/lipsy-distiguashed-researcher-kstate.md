Congratulations, Lipsy!

For the third time, Lipsy has been awarded the Distinguished Researcher Award by the Computer Science Department at Kansas State University. This recognition celebrates her remarkable research contributions and publications in prestigious conferences such as ICRA and EMSOFT.

Each year, the Computer Science Department at Kansas State University honors faculty and researchers who publish in top-ranked CSRankings venues, highlighting their excellence in advancing computer science research.

In addition to these achievements, Lipsy has published several other papers in leading journals and conferences over the past year, further strengthening her ![research portfolio](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=H3MSSaQAAAAJ&hl=en) research portfolio and impact in the field.

We extend our warmest congratulations to Lipsy for her continued success and dedication to research excellence!
