What Drives Us

Ensuring that AI-powered systems are trustworthy, safe, and accountable is essential for their deployment in high-stakes environments like robotics and autonomous platforms. As AI becomes increasingly integrated into cyber-physical systems, we focus on correctness, robustness, and safety under uncertainty, grounded in mathematical rigor and formal guarantees.
